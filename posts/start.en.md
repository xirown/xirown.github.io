I spent a long while wondering what it means to make one more blog. I already had several places to write, and most of them sat half empty. Starting was always easy; continuing was the hard part. So this time I decided to do it a little differently — not to write well, but to keep leaving something behind.

## A place where you don't have to write well

When I post on social media, my sentences oddly get shorter. In places where I don't know who will read, explanations grow long; in places full of people I know, words shrink. Both are fun, but there's a kind of writing I want to keep somewhere in between.

This is that place. Writing that doesn't need polishing for an audience, and doesn't need to reach a conclusion. A spot to set down whatever is on my mind, once every few days.

> A piece of writing is not the result of settled thoughts, but a trace of the settling.

I can't remember where I read that, but I wanted it to be the first sentence of this blog.

## What I want to keep here

- **Daily** — walks, things I bought, meals, conversations overheard
- **Thoughts** — questions that begin with a sentence I read, what I'm chewing on lately
- **Work & Study** — things I made, things I learned, attempts that failed

Three categories feel like enough. The more finely you divide things, the more you hesitate when it's time to write. If it's ambiguous, it can go under Thoughts.

## Starting slowly

The name is Pureripple. Clear water. I didn't choose it for a grand meaning — I just liked the saying that a small ripple, kept flowing, changes the shape of the shore.

Writing the first post took thirty minutes. Twenty of those went to choosing the name. That's fine. Beginnings are like that.

## After the first post

Writing the first post taught me something: the hardest part of starting a blog wasn't the writing, it was deciding what this site should look like. I changed the colour three times and the typeface five times. In the end I came back to the cream paper and dark letters I had picked at the very start.

So there isn't much decoration here. Instead I removed as much as I could that gets in the way of reading. One photograph, two or three colours, letters for the body and the headings. Maybe that simplicity is why it lasts.

## Ahead

I want to write at least once a week. If a week slips by, I've decided it's enough to sit down again the next week. The way to keep a blog for a long time seems to be this kind of ease, not resolve.

It's all right if I'm the only one reading. The first reader is always the future me.
