> This is a statement I wrote on 17 November 2026, just before sitting the 2027 CSAT, lightly edited here. The facts and figures are from that day; the argument and the wording are kept as they were.

Reports of cheating with wearable devices — AI smart glasses, pinhole cameras — kept surfacing, and calls to postpone the CSAT grew louder. The Ministry of Education said it was not considering a delay. The question is what that decision rests on.

## What can be done in two days

At the National Assembly's education committee on 16 November, the ministry's position was irresponsible. It answered that no measure could completely prevent cheating, citing the limits of technology, but did not say what could be done instead. Insisting on the schedule without establishing whether any effective measure can be in place within two days — is that a decision for 550,000 students, or a decision for administrative convenience?

Before and after the hearing, admissions commentators and private-academy instructors also demanded a delay or an emergency response. Even on behalf of students who cannot speak for themselves, we should ask whether the "fairness" that curriculum reform and crackdowns on the private-education cartel have claimed to protect is real.

## Why it has survived as "the fairest method"

Since it was introduced in 1993, the CSAT has survived criticism that it is an outdated system, always on the grounds that it is the fairest. Instead of changing the system, the government revised curricula and cracked down on the private-education cartel to keep it, and society kept its eyes on the highest scorers alone, inside the narrative that anyone can rise from poverty. Meanwhile students' anxiety became a product for the private-education market, and admissions became a vast industry. In a society where university is the default, the CSAT became a national event that even adjusts flight schedules.

## What a single multiple-choice answer decides

But does the CSAT really reward ability and effort fairly? Except for the maths essay section, every question is multiple choice with five options, and one question can separate a grade and a university's ranking. Students trading "guessing strategy" lectures right before the exam, trusting luck, is the answer. Our society still covers that unfairness with the phrase "luck is also ability." We are angry when a competitor's luck pushes our grade down, and no one raises a question when our own luck pushes it up.

## Beyond the debt revolt, an education revolt

It is time to go beyond a revolt over debt to an education revolt. The system that a competitive, meritocratic society has firmly kept in place must be overturned.

Of course, blocking cheating in the exam immediately ahead comes first, and if that is not possible, every option including a delay must be examined. But anti-cheating measures alone cannot be the fundamental solution for a fair society. Every alternative — the IB, oral assessment, and others — should be considered. In an age when technology advances frighteningly fast, the way secondary education assesses students, national licensing exams included, has to change.

## Why I am sitting this exam

I am not sitting the CSAT to comply with an unreasonable system. It is because I have a dream to reach and a work I want to do. The suffocating competition we endure must not be passed down to the next generation. Do not turn students' dreams into political sentiment and corporate profit. The tragedy of meritocracy has to end with our generation.

## What we demand of the education authorities

- Publish an effective plan to prevent CSAT cheating immediately. If you cannot guarantee one within two days, examine every option, including a delay.
- Publicly verify whether a single five-option test that decides grades and university rankings is a fair reward for ability and effort.
- Begin now the discussion to overhaul an admissions system that entrenches infinite competition and every-person-for-themselves individualism.

17 November 2026

Bae Yeon-gyeong, a student sitting the 2027 CSAT
